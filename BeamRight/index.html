Will it work with your TV?

BeamRight is built specifically for Chromecast and Google TV — and only those. We'd rather tell you now than have you find out after buying.

WORKS Supported devices

  • Chromecast with Google TV (HD & 4K)
  • Chromecast Ultra
  • Chromecast (1st, 2nd & 3rd generation)
  • Google TV streamers
  • TVs with Google Cast built-in (many Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips)

NOT YET Not supported on their own

  • Apple TV (your Mac already does this — it's AirPlay)
  • Roku
  • Amazon Fire TV / Fire Stick

Check in 10 seconds: On your TV's home screen or settings, look for the “Google Cast built-in” / “Chromecast built-in” logo — or check whether your phone can already cast to it. If yes, BeamRight will work.

FAQ

Good questions

Will it work on my Samsung / LG TV?

Not on its own — Samsung and LG don't use Google Cast. But plug a ~$30 Chromecast with Google TV into an HDMI port and BeamRight works perfectly. Quickest test: if your phone can already cast to your TV, so can BeamRight.

Do I need to install anything on my TV?

No. Nothing. As long as it's a Chromecast or Google TV device, you only install BeamRight on your Mac.

Does the sound come through too?

Yes — that's the whole point. BeamRight sends your full Mac audio in sync, including through soundbars and receivers, not just the TV's built-in speakers.

Is there any lag?

There's a natural 2–5 second delay over Wi-Fi. Great for movies, video, slideshows and presentations — but not built for fast-action gaming.

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, yours forever, with free updates. Start with the 7-day free trial — every feature unlocked — and only buy if it works for you.
