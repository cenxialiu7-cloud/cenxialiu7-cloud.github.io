FAQ · 常見問題
Why do reminders stop when I'm still using my phone?

Two things need to be true for reminders to keep coming while you're using your phone with the main app closed: (1) you've authorized Screen Time in Settings, and (2) you've selected at least one app in the app picker (the apps you actually tend to scroll — e.g. social media, browser). Without a selection, iOS never tells SleepGuard that you're still awake and using your phone, so no reminder can fire.

要在主 App 沒開的情況下持續收到提醒，需要兩個條件：①在設定授權螢幕使用時間 ②在 App 選擇器裡至少選一個你真的會滑的 App（例如社群 App、瀏覽器）。沒有選任何 App 的話，iOS 不會告訴睡眠守護者你還醒著在用手機，提醒自然就不會觸發。

Why does the reminder stop the instant I lock my screen — but is that reliable?

That's by design: SleepGuard is built to not wake you up. It sends reminders in short batches (a few at a time), and locking your screen cancels anything still pending. On devices with a passcode set, this happens within about 10 seconds. Without a passcode, iOS doesn't reliably notify apps when the screen locks, so a batch may finish playing out (a handful of notifications) before it stops.

這是刻意設計的：睡眠守護者的目標是不吵醒你。提醒以小批次發送，鎖屏會取消還沒發出的部分。 裝置有設密碼時，約 10 秒內就會停止。沒設密碼的話，iOS 不會可靠地通知 App「螢幕鎖了」， 所以可能會把當批（幾則）發完才停。

What's the most reliable way to use SleepGuard?

Open the app (or tap the bedtime notification, or say "Hey Siri, start tonight's guard") once before bed. iOS strictly limits what any app can do fully in the background, so this "arm it once, right before bed" step is what makes reminders and monitoring most reliable that night. We'd rather tell you this plainly than promise background magic iOS won't actually deliver.

睡前開一下 App（或點一下就寢提醒、或說「嘿 Siri，開始今晚守護」）。iOS 嚴格限制 App 在背景能做的事， 所以「睡前手動啟動一次」是讓當晚提醒與監測最穩定的做法。我們寧可誠實講清楚，也不想承諾 iOS 做不到的背景魔法。

I can't find SleepGuard in Control Center.

Control Center controls require iOS 18.0 or later. If your iPhone is on iOS 17, this option won't appear — everything else (Siri, Shortcuts, the widget, notifications) still works normally.

控制中心控制項需要 iOS 18.0（含）以上。若你的 iPhone 是 iOS 17，這個選項不會出現—— 其餘功能（Siri、捷徑、小工具、通知）都正常運作，不受影響。

Screen Time authorization fails, or the guard won't arm.

The most common cause: your sleep window is shorter than 15 minutes. This is an iOS platform requirement for scheduling this kind of monitoring — the app will show a message explaining this if your window is too short. Make sure Bedtime and Wake Time are set at least 15 minutes apart, then try starting the guard again.

最常見原因：你的睡眠時段短於15 分鐘。這是 iOS 排程這類監測的平台限制—— 時段太短時 App 會顯示說明訊息。請確認入睡與起床時間至少間隔 15 分鐘，再重新啟動守護。

The lock screen countdown (Live Activity) isn't showing up.

Two checks: first, enable it in Settings → Lock Screen Countdown (it's off by default so it never appears unwanted). Second, Live Activities can only be started while the app is in the foreground — so it needs you to open the app (or use one of the one-tap entry points) once at the start of your sleep window.

兩個檢查點：第一，先在設定 → 鎖屏倒數開啟它（預設關閉，不會不請自來）。 第二，Live Activity 只能在 App 前景時啟動，所以需要你在睡眠時段開始時開一下 App（或用一鍵入口）。

How do I switch the app's language?

Go to Settings → Language and choose "Follow System", "English", or "繁體中文". The change applies immediately — no restart needed — and also updates the language used by notifications, the widget, and Siri responses.

到設定 → 語言選擇「跟隨系統」、「English」或「繁體中文」。 立即生效，不需重啟 App，通知、小工具與 Siri 回覆的語言也會一併更新。

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app. SleepGuard has no server and no account, so everything — your schedule, sleep records, custom messages, and photo — lives only on your device. Uninstalling removes it all immediately; there's nothing left for us to delete on your behalf. See our Privacy Policy for details.

刪除 App 即可。睡眠守護者沒有伺服器也不需要帳號，所有資料——時段設定、睡眠紀錄、自訂訊息、照片——都只存在你的裝置上。 解除安裝會立刻清空全部資料，沒有任何伺服器端資料需要我們代為刪除。詳見隱私政策。

Notifications aren't arriving at all.

Check Settings app → Notifications → SleepGuard and confirm notifications are allowed. Also confirm the guard is actually started (the big button on the Home tab) and that the current time falls within your configured sleep window.

check 系統「設定」App → 通知 → 睡眠守護者，確認通知權限已開啟。 也請確認守護已啟動（首頁大按鈕），以及現在時間確實落在你設定的睡眠時段內。
