1. Summary

SleepGuard is a personal bedtime reminder app. It has no backend server and makes zero network requests. Every piece of data the app touches — your sleep schedule, custom reminder messages, nightly records, and optional photo — is stored only on your own iPhone. We (the developer) never see it, and it is never sent anywhere.

睡眠守護者是個人睡前提醒 App。它沒有後端伺服器， 也不會發出任何網路請求。App 接觸到的所有資料——你的睡眠時段、 自訂提醒訊息、每晚紀錄、選用的頭像照片——全部只存在你自己的 iPhone 上。我們（開發者）永遠看不到這些資料，也不會被傳送到任何地方。

2. What Data Is Stored, and Where

The following data is stored locally on your device only, using Apple's SwiftData framework and a local App Group container shared between the app and its extensions:

  • Your sleep schedule (bedtime / wake time), reminder interval, and pause settings
  • Custom reminder messages you write, if any
  • Nightly sleep records — detected bedtime and whether you met your goal (used to calculate your streak and calendar history)
  • An optional photo you choose to use as a reminder avatar, processed on-device with Apple's Vision framework for face-cropping
  • If you enable Screen Time: which of your own apps you selected in the system's app picker

以下資料只儲存在你的裝置本機，使用 Apple 的 SwiftData 框架與 App 和其擴充功能共用的本機 App Group 容器：

  • 你的睡眠時段（入睡／起床時間）、提醒間隔、暫停設定
  • 你自己寫的自訂提醒訊息（如果有）
  • 每晚睡眠紀錄——偵測到的就寢時間與是否達標（用來計算連續紀錄與月曆歷史）
  • 你選用作為提醒頭像的照片（如果有），由 Apple Vision 框架在裝置端做人臉裁切處理
  • 若你開啟螢幕使用時間功能：你在系統 App 選擇器中選取的自己的 App

3. Screen Time / Family Controls

SleepGuard offers an optional feature that uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework to detect when you are still using your own phone during your sleep window, so it can deliver a reminder even if the app itself has been suspended by iOS. This is self-monitoring for your own use — not parental control, and not monitoring of another person's device.

  • SleepGuard does not use this capability to block, shield, or restrict any app.
  • SleepGuard does not read, store, or transmit which specific apps you selected — the system gives us only opaque, anonymous tokens that never leave your device.
  • This entire feature is optional. If you don't grant Screen Time authorization, the app still works — it simply falls back to reminders scheduled when you unlock your phone.

睡眠守護者提供一個選用功能，使用 Apple 的螢幕使用時間（Family Controls）框架，偵測你自己在睡眠時段是否還在使用自己的手機， 即使 App 本身已被 iOS 暫停，也能送出提醒。這是你對自己使用習慣的自我監督——不是家長監控，也不是監控其他人的裝置。

  • 睡眠守護者不會用這個功能封鎖、限制或屏蔽任何 App。
  • 睡眠守護者不會讀取、儲存或傳送你選了哪些 App——系統只提供不記名的匿名代碼，且這些代碼永遠不會離開你的裝置。
  • 整個功能都是選用的。若不授權螢幕使用時間，App 仍可正常運作，只是改為在你解鎖手機時補排提醒。

4. Notifications

All reminders are local notifications scheduled directly on your device. SleepGuard does not use push notifications and does not need a server to deliver them. Notifications may use the Time Sensitive interruption level so a bedtime reminder can break through Focus / Do Not Disturb — you can stop them at any time from the notification itself, in the app, via Siri, Control Center, or a widget.

所有提醒都是直接排程在你裝置上的本地通知。睡眠守護者不使用推播通知，也不需要伺服器來發送。通知可能使用「時效性通知」層級， 讓睡前提醒能穿透專注模式／勿擾模式——你隨時可以透過通知本身、App 內、Siri、控制中心或小工具停止它們。

5. What We Don't Do

  • No account, sign-up, or login of any kind
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No cookies (this app has no web component)
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to anyone — because nothing ever leaves your device

我們不做以下事情：

  • 不需要任何形式的帳號、註冊或登入
  • 沒有分析或當機回報 SDK
  • 沒有廣告或廣告聯播網
  • 沒有任何第三方 SDK
  • 沒有 cookie（本 App 沒有網頁元件）
  • 不會出售、分享或傳送任何資料給任何人——因為資料根本不會離開你的裝置

6. Data Deletion

Since all data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes all of it immediately — there is nothing stored on any server for us to delete on your behalf.

因為所有資料只存在你的裝置上，刪除 App 就會立刻清除所有資料——沒有任何伺服器端的資料需要我們代為刪除。

7. Children's Privacy

SleepGuard is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone at all.

睡眠守護者分級為 4+，不會蒐集任何人的資料，包含兒童——因為它本來就不蒐集任何人的資料。
